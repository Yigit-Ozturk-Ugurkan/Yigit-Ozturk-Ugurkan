About Me

I am a Software Engineering student at Ankara University with a focus on artificial intelligence, computer vision and backend development. My projects include YOLO-based vehicle detection and classification, OCR document extraction, and applications that combine language models with retrieval and structured data.

I work mainly with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL and MongoDB, and build web interfaces with React. My recent work includes payroll document review, Telegram support workflows and website change monitoring. I also have project experience with Keycloak, JWT and role-based access control.

I enjoy connecting models to usable applications: designing APIs, organizing data, testing behavior and documenting the limits of a system alongside its results.
